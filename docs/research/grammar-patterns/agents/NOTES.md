# Batch notes for later writers and reviewers
- b13: `noni-mokuteki` duplicates `noni-purpose` (b08 owns purpose のに); the reviewer rejects it unless it differs. `ba-noni` must not cover ばよかったのに or たらよかったのに (`ba-yokatta`, b06, owns them).
- b21: `ni-tsuki` must not include につきまして; `ni-tsuite` (b09) owns it as its polite form.
- b22: `o-tsuujite` owns を通じて and 通じ (UniDic lexeme 通ずる) only; `o-tooshite` (b11) owns を通して (lexeme 通す).
- b18: `ni-kakete-mo` (b14) shipped and owns にかけても and the oath にかけて after a closed noun set (命, 名誉, 名, 誇り, 意地, 威信, 神). If `ni-kakete-wa` keeps にかけても ("when it comes to"), its guard must exclude those nouns.
- b28: `nashi` (b16) owns なしで, なしでは, なしの and なしだ. `nashi-ni` keeps only なしに and なしには.
- b23: `sou-ni-nai` keeps そうにもない (sou-nimo-nai was rejected as its duplicate) and must not list そうもない, which `sou-mo-nai` (b12) owns.
